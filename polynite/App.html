Wake Nanite

Wake Nanite is a virtual-mesh viewer running directly in your web browser on WebGPU.

It builds a Nanite-style cluster LOD hierarchy on top of meshoptimizer and renders the selected cut live: clusters, LOD levels, error heat, normals, depth, UVs and albedo.

Load one of the bundled models or drop your own .glb, .gltf, .obj or .wmesh file into the window.
